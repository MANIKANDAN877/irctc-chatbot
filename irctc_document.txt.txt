
IRCTC CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

Company:
IRCTC (Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation) is a Government of India enterprise that provides online railway ticket booking, catering, tourism, and related services.

========================
TICKET BOOKING
========================

Q: How can I book a train ticket?
A: Log in to your IRCTC account, search for trains, select a train and class, enter passenger details, make payment, and receive a booking confirmation.

Q: What documents are required during travel?
A: Passengers should carry a valid government-issued photo ID.

Q: Can I book tickets for others?
A: Yes, provided correct passenger details are entered.

Q: What is e-ticket?
A: An electronic ticket booked through the IRCTC website or mobile application.

Q: What is i-ticket?
A: A physical ticket delivered to the passenger's address where available.

========================
ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
========================

Q: How do I create an IRCTC account?
A: Visit the IRCTC website or app and complete the registration process.

Q: I forgot my password.
A: Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page.

Q: How do I update my profile?
A: Log in and use the profile management section.

========================
CANCELLATION AND REFUNDS
========================

Q: How can I cancel my ticket?
A: Log in, go to Booked Tickets, select the ticket, and choose Cancel Ticket.

Q: When will I receive my refund?
A: Refund timelines depend on the payment method and railway rules.

Q: Can a partially used ticket be cancelled?
A: Applicable rules depend on railway cancellation policies.

========================
WAITLIST AND RAC
========================

Q: What is RAC?
A: Reservation Against Cancellation allows travel with shared accommodation until full berth allocation.

Q: What is a waitlisted ticket?
A: A ticket awaiting confirmation due to unavailable seats.

Q: How can I check PNR status?
A: Use the PNR enquiry feature on the IRCTC platform.

========================
PAYMENTS
========================

Q: Which payment methods are accepted?
A: UPI, net banking, debit cards, credit cards, and supported wallets.

Q: My payment was deducted but ticket not booked.
A: Wait for system reconciliation. If booking fails, the refund is generally processed automatically according to applicable timelines.

========================
TOURISM SERVICES
========================

Q: Does IRCTC provide tour packages?
A: Yes, IRCTC offers domestic and international tourism packages.

Q: Does IRCTC offer hotel booking?
A: Yes, hotel booking services may be available through IRCTC platforms.

========================
MEALS AND CATERING
========================

Q: Can meals be ordered online?
A: Yes, meal ordering services may be available for selected trains and stations.

Q: Can I choose meal preferences?
A: Availability depends on train and service provider options.
